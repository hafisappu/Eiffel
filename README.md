# Eiffel Home — Digital Housewarming Invitation Website

A luxury, minimal, and aesthetic digital invitation website created for the housewarming ceremony and reception of **Eiffel Home**.

Built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide React.

---

## ✨ Features & Design Highlights

- **Opening Reveal Experience**:
  - Full-screen invitation envelope animation.
  - Arabic Bismillah calligraphy (`بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ`).
  - Animated Islamic arch outline drawn slowly in muted gold.
  - Elegant cursive typography for "Eiffel Home" with subtitle *"A New Beginning, A New Blessing"*.
  - Floating gold dust particles and subtle paper textures.
  - Refined "View Invitation" action with a delicate celebratory shimmer and smooth unveiling transition.

- **Main Invitation Card**:
  - Warm ivory card (`#FAF7F0`) with light beige borders (`#DDD0BB`) and delicate corner ornaments.
  - Host names: **Mr. Muneer T. K.** & **Mrs. Hisana K. P.**
  - Highlighted family name in cursive typography: **Zahy Muneer**.
  - Heartfelt invitation message.

- **Home Showcase**:
  - Architectural villa photograph with warm beige limestone facade and serene ambient glow.
  - Refined typography and delicate gold accents.

- **Event Details & Interactive Utilities**:
  - **Date**: Tuesday, 22 December 2026
  - **Time**: 3:00 PM onwards (At Our Residence)
  - **Address**: Mannankavu, Near Kerafed Junction, Naduvannur
  - **Live Countdown Timer**: Calculating days, hours, minutes, and seconds to the event.
  - **Get Directions**: One-tap navigation opening Google Maps with the exact address.
  - **Call Us**: Primary contact button (`tel:9037624200`) and secondary contact button (`tel:9037443278`).
  - **Add to Calendar**: Instant Google Calendar event integration.

- **Best Compliments**:
  - Delicate divider with gold arabesque medallion.
  - Compliments from **Kadeeja Alikutty**, **Rinshad T. K.**, and **Sargam Family**.
  - Closing message: *"We look forward to welcoming you and sharing this special occasion with you."*
  - Replay Opening and Share Invitation buttons.

- **Peaceful Audio Ambiance**:
  - Pure Web Audio API ambient soundscape (warm pentatonic tones).
  - Floating toggle button without requiring heavy external audio files.
